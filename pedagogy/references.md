## Books
1. Foley, J. D., van Dam, A., Feiner, S. K., & Hughes, J. F. (1995). Computer Graphics: Principles and Practice. Addison-Wesley Professional.
   - Comprehensive coverage of 3D transformations and matrices
   - Detailed explanation of rotation matrices and quaternions

2. Shirley, P., & Marschner, S. (2009). Fundamentals of Computer Graphics. A K Peters/CRC Press.
   - Clear explanation of transformation matrices
   - Practical examples of 3D transformations

3. Angel, E., & Shreiner, D. (2012). Interactive Computer Graphics: A Top-Down Approach with Shader-Based OpenGL. Pearson.
   - Modern approach to 3D graphics
   - Detailed coverage of transformation pipelines

## Academic Papers
1. Shoemake, K. (1985). "Animating Rotation with Quaternion Curves." ACM SIGGRAPH Computer Graphics, 19(3), 245-254.
   - Foundational paper on quaternion-based rotation
   - Smooth interpolation of rotations

2. Goldman, R. (2009). "Understanding Quaternions." Graphical Models, 73(2), 21-49.
   - Comprehensive explanation of quaternions
   - Applications in 3D rotation
