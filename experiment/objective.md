**1. Understanding Transformation Basics:** Explore fundamental concepts of 2D transformations, including translation, rotation, scaling, and their combinations. Participants will grasp how these transformations alter the position, orientation, and size of graphical elements.

**2. Matrix Representation:** Delve into the matrix representation of 2D transformations. Participants will learn to construct transformation matrices for translation, rotation, and composite transformations, understanding their application in computer graphics.

**3. Visualization and Analysis:** Gain practical experience in visualizing and analyzing the effects of 2D transformations on graphical elements. Participants will observe how different transformations impact shape, orientation, and spatial relationships.

**4. Application in Computer Graphics:** Apply learned concepts to solve problems in computer graphics. Participants will develop skills to implement and integrate 2D transformations into software applications for animation, visualization, and simulation.

These objectives aim to equip participants with a solid understanding of 2D transformations, empowering them to effectively manipulate and render graphical elements in digital environments.
