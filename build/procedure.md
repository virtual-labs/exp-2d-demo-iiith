## Introduction to Interactive Simulation:

* This experiment introduces hierarchical transformations, demonstrating their effects on four different shapes: Cube, Tetrahedron, Dodecahedron, and Octahedron. Each shape undergoes a series of animated transformations.

## Sequential Transformation Animation:

- Begin with a scaling transformation applied to each shape, followed by a rotation, translation. These transformations are visually animated.
- Use the slider to control and animate these transformations sequentially on each shape.
